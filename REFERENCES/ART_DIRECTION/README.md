# Art Direction References

Approved V1 visual references are present. The desired direction is **Stylized Premium Fantasy / Premium Toy-Fantasy**: polished forms, controlled color, readable silhouettes, and collectible appeal. The master sheet establishes warm sanctuary architecture, elemental world variety, Baby/mount/guardian contrasts, and dark fantasy GUI panels with gold trim.

Baby dragons can be cute; mounts impressive; guardians larger and intimidating. Avoid hyper-realism, an exclusively extremely childish/chibi presentation, inconsistent asset packs, and generic low-quality AI clutter. The sanctuary may evoke Dragon Village collection games; tile-by-tile city-building is not the core loop. Exact palette values, Strength/Luck stats, selling, and image-only services are not canon.
