# Progression

Authority: [MASTER_GAME_SPEC.md](../../MASTER_GAME_SPEC.md), sections 9, 20, and 22.

## Rider Progression

Permanent universal Speed, Stamina, and Boost multipliers plus Carry Capacity. Confirmed Carry levels allow 1, 2, and 3 eggs; multiplier curves and prices remain TBD.

## Dragon Collection Progression

Collect increasingly rare dragons from harder Wild Nests. Rarity, Element, and Growth Stage are independent. Exact rosters and probabilities remain TBD; owned collections are safe from direct player theft.

## Dragon Growth Progression

Baby → Young/Juvenile → Adult through Feeding. Food grants Growth Points; normal gameplay Coins primarily buy food in the sanctuary Shop. Baby is not a normal mount; Young/Juvenile and Adult are rideable. Growth preserves rarity and element. The tutorial gives enough free Starter Food for first rideability quickly.

## World Progression

Stronger rider + better dragon + higher growth stage allow farther travel and successful raids of harder nests. Later areas generally increase distance, elevation, guardian difficulty, egg quality, rarity potential, and escape difficulty. Avoid unnecessary hard level walls and a perfectly vertical nest tower.

The TWO highest-tier nests use 600-second per-slot respawn after theft; standard nests use 300 seconds. Untouched eggs remain available. Exact nest identities/tier ordering and pools remain TBD.

## Meta Loop

Steal → Hatch → Feed → Grow → Ride → Earn → Upgrade → Go farther → Steal rarer eggs.

The sanctuary is a Dragon Sanctuary/Dragon Village collection hub, not a tile-by-tile city-builder. Physical return distance is part of chase difficulty; Fast Travel/teleportation in images is not approved gameplay. The objective is escape, not guardian kills.
