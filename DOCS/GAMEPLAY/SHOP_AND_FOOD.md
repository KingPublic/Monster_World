# Shop and Food System

> Current MVP sells Basic Dragon Food for 20 Coins; each gives 50 Growth. Successful Egg delivery gives 50 Coins and tutorial completion gives 75. First hatch grants two free Food. Final economy tuning and additional products remain deferred.


Authority: [MASTER_GAME_SPEC.md](../../MASTER_GAME_SPEC.md), sections 16 and 20.

The Player Nest contains a **physical Shop**. One of its primary functions is selling **Dragon Food**, primarily purchased using normal gameplay **Coins**.

## Food

Dragon Food grants Growth Points. Feeding gives players reasons to earn Coins, return to their Sanctuary, care for collected dragons, and grow Babies into rideable Young/Juvenile and mature Adult dragons.

## Current Shop Categories

### Dragon Food

Core progression category. Possible types may eventually include Starter Food, Basic Food, Better Food, and element-themed treats. Exact catalog, points, prices, icons, and any other values are TBD. Onboarding provides enough free Starter Food for the first rideable stage.

### Utility

Future optional gameplay-support items. Do not finalize the catalog or mechanics now.

### Cosmetics

Future customization category, not required for current MVP gameplay.
