# Monster World visual cohesion and playability polish — 2026-10-11

User authorization: inspect both supplied screen recordings and improve the existing game, models, motion, Dragon family continuity, Guardians and spaciousness/cohesion of the city. Preserve source files, references, user saves and all existing uncommitted work. No staging, commit, push or deployment.

## Evidence and intended result

The 66.1-second and 28.7-second recordings show bare slab terraces, a carpet of noisy road texture, stretched building silhouettes, ornamental objects with conflicting scale, large empty green surroundings with single-cone trees/mountains, a camera inside facades, a stiff player/Dragon, and green procedural Guardians/Babies alongside the brown downloaded Young. This is functional asset placement but does not establish visual readiness.

The result should be an inhabited fantasy sanctuary: broad connected avenues and gardens, a clear central landmark, readable service districts, open landing space, buildings with natural proportions, framed views, consistent lighting/materials, richer nearby terrain and distant atmospheric silhouettes. Gameplay remains Egg theft and physical escape, hatch/feed/growth, riding/flight, Sanctuary services and local save. No combat, paid systems, regions, quests, multiplayer or economy redesign.

## Decisions

- Recompose the existing Sanctuary around a shared layout consumed by rendering and traversal. Keep the original 145-unit safe perimeter and nest/world distances; use its space more fully, with low terraces and broad stairs rather than tall enclosing slabs. Extend decorative outskirts only beyond the protected city.
- Imported buildings use uniform scale, explicit rotations and measured footprints. Paving belongs to streets/courtyards; gardens, stone borders, vegetation, walls and water connect districts. Remove redundant placeholder towers/statues once real assets replace them.
- Use the Mountain family for owned Baby, Young and larger Guardian parents, preserving the saved rarity/element identity. Baby proportions are authored from the verified skeleton (head/body/wing differences), not a source-supplied Baby or a uniformly shrunken mature model. This avoids changing species at growth. Guardians keep existing chase/capture/return rules.
- Drive motion with continuous blending, phase/cadence and coordinated body, head, tail, wings, folded legs, foot contact and saddle/rider pose. Retain independent physics/mount/carry roots. Source animations and authored runtime motions remain distinguished.
- Improve the existing Cartoon Boy gait and secondary movement and resolve camera obstruction against actual city footprints. Keep controls, speed limits, economy and save schema intact.
- Add suitable audited NPC/ambient/prop assets where they serve the composition. Broken or excessive sources are repaired/optimized only when their result can be visually verified; do not force every pack into the city or describe all 19 as ready.
- Compare before/after matched camera views and short motion sequences, not just successful network requests. Verify fresh/save gameplay, physical city routes, Guardian visuals, Baby/Young continuity, carried flight, touch emulation, failures and cleanup.

## Acceptance and honest limits

The new game must show substantive visible improvement and real downloaded Guardians, visually consistent Baby→Young, smooth motion, discoverable services with physically navigable paths, camera clearance and open landing space. Source hashes must match. Tests/build passing are necessary, but do not establish artistic approval, physical-device certification, universal performance or external licensing clearance. All unpromoted sources retain their exact blockers in the asset reports.
