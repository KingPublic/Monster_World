# Player Nest / Sanctuary

Authority: [MASTER_GAME_SPEC.md](../../MASTER_GAME_SPEC.md), sections 16 and 18–20.

Each player has their own home/safe zone, hatch area, collection area, mount selection area, and progression hub. The approved visual direction is a compact **Dragon Sanctuary or Dragon Village**, evoking dragon-collection/city fantasy without making tile-by-tile building the core game.

## Sanctuary / Village Areas

The sanctuary may contain Hatchery, Dragon Collection, Mount selection, Rider Upgrade, and physical Shop areas, decorative habitats, and spaces where owned dragons visibly live. The hub can feel social and support collection/progression.

Secured eggs hatch as small Babies living here. Feeding grants Growth Points and grows them into rideable Young/Juvenile, then Adult dragons. The Shop primarily sells Dragon Food for normal gameplay Coins. Free Starter Food quickly reaches the first rideable stage during onboarding; no grinding or premium gate is added.

Collections support inspecting/comparing dragons, their independent Rarity/Element/Growth Stage, stage-adjusted Base Stats, rider-modified Final Stats, rideability, and equipped state. Owned eggs and dragons cannot be stolen by other players.
