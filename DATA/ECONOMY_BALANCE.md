# Economy Balance Planning

Authority: [MASTER_GAME_SPEC.md](../MASTER_GAME_SPEC.md), sections 20 and 27.

Normal gameplay **Coins** are approved as the primary currency for buying Dragon Food in the physical Player Nest Shop. Food grants Growth Points and supports feeding-based Baby → rideable Young/Juvenile → Adult growth. The tutorial grants enough free Starter Food for first rideability quickly.

| Area | Confirmed direction | Values / rules still TBD |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coins | Normal gameplay food purchases | Income sources, reward amounts, rates |
| Dragon Food | Core Shop progression category | Final catalog, prices, Growth Points |
| Dragon Growth | Feed to fill Growth Progress and reach next stage | Baby → Juvenile and Juvenile → Adult thresholds; stage stat scaling |
| Tutorial food | Free Starter Food sufficient for first rideability | Grant quantity balanced with points/first threshold |
| Rider upgrades | Universal Speed/Stamina/Boost multipliers and Carry 1/2/3 | Upgrade prices and multiplier curves |
| Hatching | Quick tutorial hatch into Baby | Later timings; no image-only paid speed-ups |
| Utility | Future optional support category | Items and mechanics |
| Cosmetics | Future customization; not required for current MVP gameplay | Items and any pricing |
| Monetization | No mandatory pay-to-win or removal of the core loop | Exact systems/products require later explicit approval |

Starter Food, Basic Food, Better Food, and element-themed treats are possible types, not a finalized priced catalog. Premium currency, passive income, timed boosters, auto-collect, happiness systems, and exact products/prices from Shop/collection images are not approved.

No future balancing numbers are invented. Approved numeric rules are stored elsewhere: Carry capacity 1/2/3, V1 target 3–5 nest slots, and configurable per-slot respawn 300 seconds standard / 600 for the two highest-tier nests. All food points, thresholds, prices, and stat scaling remain TBD.
